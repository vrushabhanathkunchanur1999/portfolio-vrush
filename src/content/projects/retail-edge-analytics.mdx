scale: 400+ stores live, grown from 40+ pilots, with a 700-store rollout under contract
problem: Store operators needed footfall, trial-room, billing-counter and greeting-zone metrics from in-store RTSP camera feeds, computed on a Raspberry Pi 5 inside each store.
outcomes:
  - metric: Inference latency on Raspberry Pi 5
    value: 35% lower
    note: after moving from ONNX Runtime at FP32 to NCNN at FP16 with a 352×352 detector input
  - metric: Median inference per camera
    value: 90 ms
    note: with the Pi's four cores partitioned into dedicated NCNN pipelines
  - metric: Footfall agreement with manual counts
    value: 94%
    note: after replacing naive line-crossing with an inner-boundary rule
  - metric: Model size on Raspberry Pi 5
    value: 45% smaller
    note: FP16 quantisation of the detector and classifier
  - metric: Person detection on held-out store footage
    value: 0.90 mAP@0.5
    note: YOLOX-S trained on 25,000 annotated store-camera frames
  - metric: Crop classification accuracy on held-out footage
    value: 92%
    note: dual-head MobileNetV3-Small, demographic and customer/employee heads
